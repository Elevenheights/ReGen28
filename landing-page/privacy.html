Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

ReGen28 collects information to provide a personalized wellness experience. This includes:

  • Account Info: Email address and profile details for authentication via Firebase.
  • Wellness Data: Tracker entries, journal logs, and ritual progress.
  • AI Context: Information you provide to calibrate your AI Coach (age, focus areas, goals).
  • Images: Reference photos you upload for AI image generation. These are processed securely to visualize your transformation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to generate personalized motivational content, visualize your goals via AI, and provide wellness insights. We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Storage) with industry-standard encryption and security protocols.

AI Providers and Your Permission

AI features are optional. With your explicit permission, wellness goals, focus areas, profile details (such as age and gender), location, habit and mood records, and journal context are sent to OpenAI to generate coaching and insights. For AI visualizations, prompts and selected reference/progress photos may be processed by OpenAI and Google Vertex AI (Gemini). Photos are optional. Decline or revoke permission in Settings > AI data-sharing choices to stop new AI requests; prior processing cannot be recalled.

Provider policies: OpenAI and Google Cloud. Firebase stores your account and app data. RevenueCat manages subscription status with an app user identifier and purchase history. Apple processes payments; we do not receive payment card details. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Your Rights

Use Settings > Delete account permanently, or Manage account on the subscription screen, to delete your Firebase account, wellness records, journal entries, and uploaded/generated images. For security, sign in again immediately before deletion. Deletion does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel in Apple Settings > Subscriptions first. Apple and billing providers may retain transaction records under their own retention obligations.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@regen28.com.
