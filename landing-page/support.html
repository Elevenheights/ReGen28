ReGen28 Support

For account, billing, or app questions, email support@regen28.com. Include your app version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or private wellness records.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel an iOS subscription in Apple Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases on the ReGen28 subscription screen if you have an existing purchase. Apple handles charges and refund requests.

Privacy and account deletion

Use Settings > AI data-sharing choices to change permission for AI processing. Use Settings > Delete account permanently to remove your account and personal app data. Manage account is also available from the subscription screen. Sign in again before deletion. Account deletion does not cancel an App Store subscription.
